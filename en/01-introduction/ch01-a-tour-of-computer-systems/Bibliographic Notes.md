# Bibliographic Notes

[Back to Chapter Contents](./Chapter%201%20A%20Tour%20of%20Computer%20Systems.md)

Ritchie has written interesting firsthand accounts of the early days of C and Unix \[[91](#ref-91), [92](#ref-92)\]. Ritchie and Thompson presented the first published account of Unix [\[93\]](#ref-93). Silberschatz, Galvin, and Gagne [\[102\]](#ref-102) provide a comprehensive history of the different flavors of Unix. The GNU ([GNU Project](https://www.gnu.org/)) and Linux ([Linux Kernel Archives](https://www.kernel.org/)) Web pages have loads of current and historical information. The Posix standards are available online at [The Open Group POSIX resources](https://posix.opengroup.org/posixfaq.html).
