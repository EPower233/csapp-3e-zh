Kerrisk gives a comprehensive treatment of Unix I/O and the Linux file system [\[62\]](#ref-62). Stevens wrote the original standard reference text for Unix I/O [\[111\]](#ref-111). Kernighan and Ritchie give a clear and complete discussion of the standard I/O functions [\[61\]](#ref-61).
