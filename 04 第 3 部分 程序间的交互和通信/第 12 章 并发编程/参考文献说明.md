信号量操作是 Dijkstra 提出的 [\[31\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2031)。进度图的概念是 Coffman [\[23\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2023) 提出的，后来由 Carson 和 Reynolds [\[16\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2016) 形式化的。Courtois 等人 [\[25\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2025) 提出了读者-写者问题。操作系统教科书更详细地描述了经典的同步问题，例如哲学家进餐问题、打瞌睡的理发师问题和吸烟者问题 \[[102](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%20102), [106](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%20106), [113](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%20113)\]。Butenhof 的书 [\[15\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2015) 对 Posix 线程接口有全面的描述。Birrell [\[7\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%207) 的论文对线程编程以及线程编程中容易遇到的问题做了很好的介绍。Reinders 的书 [\[90\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2090) 描述了 C/C++ 库，简化了线程化程序的设计和实现。有一些课本讲述了多核系统上并行编程的基础知识 \[[47](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2047), [71](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2071)\]。Pugh 描述了 Java 线程通过内存进行交互的方式的缺陷，并提出了替代的内存模型 [\[88\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2088)。Gustafson 提出了替代强扩展的弱扩展加速模型 [\[43\]](../../05%20%E9%99%84%E5%BD%95%E4%B8%8E%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE.md#%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE%2043)。
